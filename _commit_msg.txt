fix(htmlRenderV8): collectTableRows flush + buildHtml freshness guard

Issue: collectTableRows silently dropped closed rows (rows ending with closing pipe).
buildHtml unconditionally loaded dist/aligned-tables.json without freshness check,
so a stale bundle could splice a different reports tables into fresh renders.

Fixes:
- collectTableRows: flush previous row on every pipe-start; trailing flush always pushes.
- loadTypedTables: parse bundle.source_generated_at.
- buildHtml: skip typed overrides when stale or missing the field; warn on stdout.

Verified: unit tests 9/9, 6/6. verifier 41/0/0.
